OUR BACKGROUND

Dakwat Space Services is a creative hub specializing in printing, photography, 3D design, graphic design, website development, and digital content solutions. Our vision is to build a space where creativity, innovation, and practical execution come together supporting designers, creators, and businesses in bringing their ideas to life. In the future, we aim to grow into a creative innovation hub, exploring new technologies such as i mmersive presentations and VR-based solutions for design and branding experiences.

Dakwat Space was founded with a simple but personal motivation. As a design student, I often experienced last-minute printing needs during late hours, and there was no accessible solution available at that time. This inspired the idea to create a 24-hour printing service that is always available, especially for students and creatives who work under tight deadlines.

Located near UiTM Puncak Alam, Dakwat Space naturally grew into a convenient solution for students and local residents. Over time, it also became a trusted creative s ervice point for the surrounding community, offering more than just printing but a full range of design and digital services.

One of the key motivations behind Dakwat Space is solving a clear gap in the market the lack of a fully operational 24-hour printing service with real staff support. We aim to provide not just convenience, but reliability, creativity, and innovation in one place.

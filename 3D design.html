3D Design

At Dakwat, our 3D Design team covers a wide range of services from concept development to high-quality rendering. We create detailed and realistic 3D models tailored for various purposes, including product visualization, architecture, advertising, and production.

Our expertise includes product modeling, furniture and object modeling, architectural and interior modeling, structural and engineering visualization, as well as character and mascot modeling. We also produce photo-realistic renders, walkthrough animations, VR/AR-ready assets, and 3D print files (STL/OBJ) for prototypes or presentations. Whether you need 3D visuals for marketing campaigns, architectural proposals, or digital experiences, Dakwat brings your ideas to life with precision, creativity, and attention to detail.

3D Modeling

Want to see your furniture concept come to life before production? At Dakwat, we can model and visualize your branded furniture in 3D giving you a realistic preview for reference, presentation, or design refinement before it’s built.
